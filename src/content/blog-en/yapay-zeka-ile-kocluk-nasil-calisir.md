“The unexamined life is not worth living.” — Socrates

AI coaching is a structured conversation that helps you express a goal more clearly, see your options and make the next step concrete. Perda aims to make the decision space easier to understand rather than present a single right answer. The coaching app therefore asks questions, organises answers and helps you hear your own thoughts more clearly instead of offering ready-made advice.

## How does a conversation begin?

A good coaching conversation starts with a strong question about your goal. Is there something you would like to change in your life? This can lead to career decisions, relationship coaching, leadership behaviours, daily habits or personal development. Although it sounds simple, the question helps gather scattered concerns into a single focus.

Many people think they know what they want, then discover different needs beneath the goal as they begin to talk. Someone seeking greater success may actually be searching for security. Someone wanting better communication may encounter an old belief about not being heard. AI-supported coaching uses structured questions to make these distinctions visible.

## What does AI do?

An AI coach generates follow-up questions based on your responses, highlights recurring thought patterns and helps organise possible actions. The conversation stays focused while your thoughts begin to separate into clearer parts. Perda centres on a coaching flow that considers feelings, goals, beliefs, options and small actions together.

The coaching app does not decide for you. Instead of saying “You should do this”, it asks questions such as “What does this option represent for you?”, “Which belief underlies this thought?” and “What could the next small step be?” Two people facing the same problem may have different needs. Each person's history, expectations, perspective and values are different.

## What happens after a session?

At Perda, AI leads the main coaching experience. After a session, you can use Keok habit support to carry the small step you identified into everyday life. You can also delete the conversation at the end of the session, keeping privacy control in your hands.

This structure distinguishes Perda from general-purpose chat tools. In an open-ended conversation, topics can drift; you may explain a great deal without clarifying what you learned or what to do next. The coaching app aims to keep the conversation on a defined path. Awareness, concrete action and a focus on solutions are treated as parts of the same experience within 7–10 questions.

The value for you is that the process is available at any time. If a message at 11 p.m. leaves you unsettled or you wake at 4 a.m. feeling anxious, you may be able to organise your thoughts without waiting. This support does not replace professional help or human relationships, but offers a practical space to gather your thoughts in the moment.
